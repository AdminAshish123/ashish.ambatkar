Step 3 & 4

Download, then paste one line

Your color and corner style are already baked into the file below.

<script src="tts-widget.js"></script>
  1. 1. Upload the downloaded tts-widget.js file to your website's files (same place as your other scripts/images).
  2. 2. Copy the one line of code above.
  3. 3. Paste it in your page's HTML exactly where you want the player to appear — for example, right above your article's headline.
  4. 4. Done. It now appears automatically on that page, and on every future article you add the same line to.
Note: this file must be hosted on your own website. We don't host it for you — that's what keeps this 100% free and fully disconnected from us after download.
